RevCheck

Two listings. Which one is the better buy?

Paste two used-car listings from mobile.bg or cars.bg. Gemma 4, an open-weight model, looks at the photos for damage and equipment. The price is checked against similar cars for sale, with a fair-price range from TabPFN. Then you get a verdict and what to ask the seller.

✓ RevCheck Reader is installed.

Install the RevCheck Reader extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

mobile.bg and cars.bg block requests from cloud servers, so RevCheck reads the listings from your own browser. The extension only fetches mobile.bg and cars.bg listing and search pages when you click Compare, without your cookies. It's about 60 lines of code.

  1. Download revcheck-reader.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Reload this page.

Read the extension's code

Your browser reads the two listings and similar cars, then sends that data to a DigitalOcean Function, which passes the photos to Gemma 4 on DigitalOcean Serverless Inference and the price data to TabPFN. Nothing is stored. Treat the result as a second opinion, not an inspection.
