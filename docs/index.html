Two listings. Which one is the better buy?

Paste two used-car listings from mobile.bg or cars.bg. Gemma 4 looks at every photo for damage and equipment, DeepSeek V4 flags the known weak spots of each engine, and TabPFN works out a fair price from similar cars for sale. Then you get a verdict and what to ask the seller. All open-weight models.

The two links go to a DigitalOcean Function, which reads the public listings through a small Cloudflare Worker. Gemma 4 and DeepSeek V4 run on DigitalOcean Serverless Inference, TabPFN on Prior Labs. Nothing is stored. Treat the result as a second opinion, not an inspection.
