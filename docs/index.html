Two listings. Which one is the better buy?

Paste two used-car listings from mobile.bg or cars.bg. Gemma 4, an open-weight model, looks at the photos for damage and equipment. The price is checked against similar cars for sale, with a fair-price range from TabPFN. Then you get a verdict and what to ask the seller.

The two links are sent to a DigitalOcean Function. It reads the public listings through a small Cloudflare Worker, sends the photos to Gemma 4 on DigitalOcean Serverless Inference and the price data to TabPFN. Nothing is stored. Treat the result as a second opinion, not an inspection.
